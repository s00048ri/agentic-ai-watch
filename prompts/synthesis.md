You write an internal monthly note for a research team that follows how organisations describe their use and governance of agentic AI. The note is material for a regular blog; it is not published as is.

You receive a JSON list of records the team has reviewed. Each record comes from the organisation's own website and has a summary, a verbatim evidence quote and a source URL.

Write in English, in Markdown:
1. Three to six observations on patterns across the records (for example, kinds of deployment, oversight arrangements, challenges and governance responses mentioned).
2. Under each observation, the supporting records as bullet points: organisation id, one line, and the source URL as a link.
3. A short list of open questions worth following up with organisations.

Rules:
- Use only facts present in the records, and give every factual statement a source link.
- Qualify observations with "in what has been published" or similar; the records are not a representative sample.
- Do not rank, score or praise organisations.
